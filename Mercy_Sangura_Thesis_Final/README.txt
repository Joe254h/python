BEFORE YOU PRINT
----------------
1. Open Mercy_Sangura_Thesis_FINAL.docx in Word.
2. Press Ctrl+A, then F9. When Word asks, choose "Update entire table".
   This fills in the page numbers in the table of contents, the list of
   tables and the list of figures, which cannot be computed outside Word.
3. Scroll through and add a page break anywhere a table breaks awkwardly
   across two pages.

HOW THE NUMBERS WERE CHECKED
----------------------------
Every figure printed in the thesis was re-derived from the .sav file and
compared against the document: 3,720 categorical table cells, 207 price and
margin figures, 93 market-structure figures, 67 sample and association
figures, 924 appendix cells, 111 count and percentage claims in the prose and
16 Kruskal-Wallis statistics. The built file is also checked for table
shading, run colour, typeface and size, caption shape, table and figure
numbering, agreement between the lists of tables and figures and the
captions, agreement between the table of contents and the headings, exhibit
references that point outside their own section, and the AI writing patterns
in Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing". No mismatches remain. The detail is in
Revision_Checklist.docx, sections C, E and F.
